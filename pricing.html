Questions

Do I need to pay to share my card?

No. Your card, QR code, NFC sharing, web card and the card scanner are free, and the people you share with never need the app.

How do I cancel Pro?

Pro renews automatically until you cancel. Cancel anytime in your Apple account's subscription settings or in Google Play, at least 24 hours before it renews. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

How does Teams billing work?

You choose the number of seats (people) when you start, and can change them anytime in the dashboard's Billing tab. Prices don't include tax where it applies.

We already have Pro members. What happens when we get Teams?

Everyone in the company gets Pro while they're an active member. Anyone with their own Pro subscription can cancel it in their app store if they no longer need it.

Is my data sold or used for ads?

No. We don't sell personal information or use it for advertising. See our Privacy Policy.
