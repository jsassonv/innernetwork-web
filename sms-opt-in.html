How guests opt in to event texts

Inner Network (Wavecode LLC, doing business as Inner Network) sends event reminder texts only to guests who opt in on their personal event invitation page, after they answer Going. This page shows the exact opt-in as guests see it.

SMS Terms · Privacy Policy

The box is never pre-checked. A guest is texted reminders only after ticking it and saving.

What happens next

  1. The guest ticks the box, enters a mobile number and taps Save.
  2. Inner Network texts reminders about that event, each ending with "Reply STOP to opt out."
  3. Replying STOP ends all Inner Network event texts to that number. Replying START turns them back on. Replying HELP gives support details.

Questions: contact@innernetwork.app. See also the SMS Terms.
