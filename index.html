FAQ

Good questions.

Who is Inner Network for?

Professionals in construction and real estate, including developers, contractors, architects, engineers, lenders, brokers and property managers.

Where do the events come from?

Events are collected from chambers, associations and industry organizers and shown in one place. Each listing links to the organizer for full details.

Do people need the app to see my card?

No. Your card opens in any phone browser from your link or QR code, and they can save your contact or send you theirs.

Can my whole team use it?

Yes. Company accounts include team members, a shared card design, email signatures, virtual backgrounds and an activity log.

Who can see my tags and notes?

Only you. Tags and notes on your contacts are private to your account.

Where can I use Inner Network?

On iPhone, on Android, and on the web at app.innernetwork.app. Everything stays in sync.
