FAQ

Good questions.

Who is Inner Network for?

Professionals in construction and real estate, including developers, contractors, architects, engineers, lenders, brokers and property managers.

Where do the events come from?

Events come from organizers' public websites and event pages. Every listing links to the organizer's own registration page. Organizers can claim, edit or remove a listing on request. See our Removal & Corrections page.

Do people need the app to see my card?

No. You can share a link or QR code to a read-only web version of your card. Anyone who opens it, including people who are not members, can see the card fields you have made visible plus your name, role, interests and social links.

Who can see my card?

Every signed-in member can see your name, role, headline, bio, company, title, industries and interests. Your email, phone numbers, websites and company email appear only if you turn the matching switch on when editing your card. See our Privacy Policy.

Who can see my tags and notes?

Only you. Private notes, tags and "where we met" on your connections are visible only to you. So are your Networking ROI records.

Where can I use Inner Network?

On iPhone and Android. Download it from the App Store or Google Play.
