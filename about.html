About Inner Network

Networking should pay off.

Inner Network is the networking platform for professionals, business owners and salespeople. Find events worth your time, share your digital card with a tap, keep every contact organized, and sign documents, all in one place.

Why we built it

"Every week I had the same three problems: figuring out where to go, connecting with the right people once I got there, and keeping track of what came of it."

Our founder spent ten years in business development and sales. The events were scattered across dozens of websites and email lists, business cards piled up in a drawer, and there was no easy way to tell which rooms were worth the time.

Inner Network brings all three into one place, so the effort you put into meeting people turns into relationships and business you can see.

Three questions, one platform

Know where to go.Business and wellness events in one calendar, with the tools to host your own.
Know who to meet.A digital business card, a link page and a network of members by industry and area.
Know what it's worth.Contacts, deals, follow-ups, meeting notes and reports that show what your networking returns.

What we believe

Your data is yours. We don't sell it, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps. You can download or delete your data at any time.

Privacy is built in. The meeting assistant turns speech into text on your own phone, so the recording is never uploaded. People only see the card details you choose to share.

Organizers stay in control. Event listings link back to the organizer's own page. Organizers can claim, correct or remove a listing whenever they want.

Everyone is welcome. Inner Network works on iPhone, Android and the web, in English and Spanish.
