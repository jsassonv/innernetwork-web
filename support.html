Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot Password" and follow the instructions sent to your email address.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Account settings within the App and select "Delete Account." This permanently removes your profile and personal information, subject to the retention terms described in our Privacy Policy. You can also request account deletion by emailing us at jsassonv@gmail.com.

How do I turn off location or push notifications?

Location and notification permissions can be managed at any time in your device's Settings app, under Inner Network. Turning these off only limits distance-based event sorting, event check-in, and reminders; the rest of the App continues to work normally.

How do I report a user or inappropriate content?

Email us at jsassonv@gmail.com with details, including the user's name or the content in question, and we'll investigate promptly.

How do I hide my event attendance or profile information from other members?

You can control what's visible on your profile card and whether your event attendance is shown to other members from your privacy settings within the App.
