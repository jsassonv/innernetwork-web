How it works

  1. Download Inner Network and create your company in Account > Company.
  2. Sign in to the web dashboard with the same account and choose Teams under Billing.
  3. Invite your team, set your company card, and connect your CRM if you like.

Questions

Do my people need to pay anything?

No. Teams covers everyone in the company, and they get Pro while they're active members.

Can members leave?

Yes, anytime. Their account and card stay theirs. Leads they collected while in the company can stay with the team.

Do you offer single sign-on (SSO)?

Yes, on the Enterprise plan, with Okta, Microsoft Entra or Google Workspace. Contact us.
